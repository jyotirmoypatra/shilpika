Our craft

Not just made.
Made meaningful.

Every Shilpika piece starts with a tradition — the painted gachkouto of a Bengali wedding, the shola topor, the alpona on a kulo — and a pair of hands that knows how to keep it alive.

We work with artisans who learned these crafts from their families, and we design for how people celebrate and live today. Slow by choice, and never quite the same twice.
